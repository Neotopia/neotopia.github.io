Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026

This site is a personal, non-commercial portfolio. For any question about your data, or to exercise your rights, you can write at any time to lisa.learning8@gmail.com.

1. Data collected

This site does not have a contact form and does not knowingly collect any directly identifying personal data. Once you consent to cookies, Google Analytics 4 automatically collects: pages viewed, clicks (categorised as interaction type, platform, page section, project name), device and browser type, approximate location (derived from an anonymised IP address), and the referring site. No advertising or social media tracking is used.

2. Purpose

This data is used solely to understand how the site is used (which sections and projects attract interest) in order to improve it, and as a demonstration of the analytics tracking implementation shown in the "Projects" section.

3. Legal basis

Processing is based on your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR), collected via the cookie banner before any audience-measurement cookie is set. You can withdraw your consent at any time (see section 7).

4. Cookies used

Only one category of cookie is used: audience measurement (Google Analytics 4). No advertising, social media, or third-party sharing cookies are set. The exact list of cookies, their names and lifetimes is available and kept up to date in the cookie consent banner.

5. Recipients and transfers outside the EU

Data collected via Google Analytics is processed by Google LLC (processor), which may process it in the United States. This transfer is governed by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. Data is not sold or shared with any other third party.

6. Retention period

Analytics data is kept for a maximum of 14 months, in line with CNIL recommendations for audience-measurement cookies. Consent choices themselves are stored for up to 12 months.

7. Your rights

Under GDPR, you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, or object to the processing of your data, and to data portability. You can withdraw your consent at any time using the "Manage my cookie preferences" button above. To exercise any of these rights, contact lisa.learning8@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the CNIL (cnil.fr).

8. Security

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages and served exclusively over HTTPS.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The date at the top of this page always reflects the latest version.
